How It Started

Two desks, one Bloomberg terminal, and a stubborn idea

In 2014, three analysts left their jobs at larger institutions convinced that retail investors deserved the same research quality as institutional desks — just without the jargon and the six-figure minimums. They rented two desks above a textile showroom in Salem, split one Bloomberg terminal, and started publishing research notes for free.

Word spread. Within eighteen months the free notes turned into a paid advisory, then a brokerage license, then a full platform. Every feature we've shipped since — screeners, SIP automation, portfolio rebalancing — traces back to a problem one of our own analysts hit first.

Today STACKLY Capital manages over ₹18,400 crore in client assets, but the founding rule hasn't changed: nothing ships until our own desk trades on it first.

Milestones

Eleven years, in the moments that mattered

A brokerage doesn't grow in a straight line — here's the version with the bumps left in.

2014

Two desks, one Bloomberg terminal

Founded in Salem by three analysts publishing free equity research notes to build trust before building a business.

2016

Brokerage license granted

SEBI registration cleared and STACKLY opened its first 10,000 trading accounts within the year.

2018

Direct mutual funds, zero commission

Launched a direct-plan SIP engine, cutting recurring costs for over 200,000 mutual fund investors overnight.

2020

Sub-10ms order execution

Co-located servers went live on both NSE and BSE, putting retail order speed on par with institutional desks.

2022

Portfolio Management Services desk opens

A dedicated PMS team began managing actively rebalanced model portfolios for high-net-worth clients.

2024

3 million investors, 42 cities

Crossed 3 million active accounts while expanding advisory desks into 42 cities across India.

2026

₹18,400 Cr+ in assets managed

Still headquartered a floor above where it started — now with a research desk four times the size.
