Questions

Everything you were about to ask

Is there really zero annual maintenance charge?

Yes — every plan, including the free Starter tier, carries zero account-opening and zero annual maintenance charges. The only cost on trading is a flat ₹20 brokerage per executed order.

What's the minimum amount to start a SIP?

SIPs start at ₹100 per month across our direct-plan mutual fund catalogue, with no lock-in beyond the fund's own exit-load schedule.

How is Portfolio Management different from a mutual fund?

PMS holds securities directly in your own demat account rather than pooling investors into a fund, giving you full transparency into every position and full control over tax events, at a ₹50 lakh minimum.

Can I switch plans later?

Yes, you can move between Starter, Growth and Wealth at any time from your dashboard — changes take effect from the next billing cycle with no penalty.

Do I get a dedicated advisor on every plan?

A dedicated SEBI-registered advisor is included from the Growth plan upward. Starter plan clients can still book paid one-off advisory sessions on demand.
